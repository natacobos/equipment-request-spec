# Write the spec for a Power Platform solution

Our team writes a short technical spec before building anything on Power Platform. The retail
operations team sent the request in `REQUEST.md`. Your job is to write the spec for the solution in
`SPEC.md`, as you would before starting to build it. You don't build anything and you don't need a
Power Platform environment.

You may use your usual AI tool; we will watch how you use it. Please think out loud.

## Files

- `REQUEST.md`: what the business asked for, and the facts we know. Read it first.
- `SPEC.md`: your spec. It has six headings; write short bullet points under each one.

## Time: 15 minutes in total

1. **Read the request (about 2 minutes).**
2. **Write the spec (about 13 minutes).** All six headings, in any order. Short bullet points are
   enough; we care about your decisions and the reasons behind them, not about long text.

If you are still on one heading three minutes after you started it, move on to the next one.
At 15 minutes we stop and you show us `SPEC.md`.
